Shri Sarwatma Dham Ashram was established by Swami Sarwatma Nand Maharaj in 1984, with a vision to create self-awareness among every individual by way of Dhyana, Bhakti (devotion) and Yoga Asana traditionally taught for thousands of years.

The ashram is located in the heart of the iconic Ram Jhula and Laxman Jhula. It is situated on the banks of the holy river Ganga with private access to alluring beaches and rocks. The gushing sound of the holy river Ganga heals you round the clock during your stay in the ashram.
